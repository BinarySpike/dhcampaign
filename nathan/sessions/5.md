# Session 5 -- Red Fanta

Fear: 7

[[toc]]

> *Nyx is not present for the game, we described his character as being taken by two warrior faeries to answer to his matriarch about recent events.  Such as his exploits outside the Drylands, his return and saving of the Train, killings at the Quarry, the Duel with "Mad Dog" Agnis Purvil, and the stopping of the train bandits*

Our heroes enter Cinderline with the reassurance that the Cinderline Train Authority will pay for lodging.

## Nothing can save these rolls

Our players see a large group of people near a main thoroughfare, waiting their turn to collect water from an essentia powered windmill.  Seeing the mistreatment from a Firbolg "guard" against a woman and her children, Cer Rantix unsuccessfully attempts to help the woman.  Confronted by the guard, Clint apologizes successfully and the heroes continue on to a mid-quality saloon named the *Steam Line Saloon* where they might dine on the terrace, the Steam Deck.

While passing a low-quality saloon overrun with riff-raff, Cer Rantix runs into **Jason** a Human Bard played by N2.

> *While running from his captors, Cer Rantix met Jason and pleaded for Jason to hide him.  Jason, being the conman he is, left Cer Rantix in a dump to be caught.  This was enough for Cer Rantix to successfully escape his captors and forever remember Jason as one of the few good people he met in the Drylands*

Happy to see his old friend, they catch up and find out that Jason is headed to the Steam Line Saloon to attend a business meeting.  They head the same way.

## Why is Clint's face so offensive??

Arriving at the Steam Line Saloon.  Jason and Clint head to a back room looking for a good card game and stumble into a business meeting between Don Fontaine (Mayor of Cinderline), a gray suited representative for Don Anderson (Mayor of Havalaugh), and supporting cast.

Seemingly offended by Clint Brook, the gray suited man and his company leave.

Cer Rantix attempts to pick-pocket the grey suited man as he leaves (success with hope) and fails without raising suspicion.  He observes that the grey suited man and Don Fontaine are wearing traditional magic thread clothing and that Don Fontaine is hiding a significantly powerful magic weapon.

Don Fontaine shakes Clint's hand and possibly casts a magic spell on him.  He then begins interrogating Clint Brook by dispelling Cer Rantix's minor illusion man and forcibly destroying his Midnight Spirit.  The heroes discuss their connection with Jessup Buchanan, providing shaky evidence with their communication orb and Cer Rantix's disguise device.  Don Fontaine apologizes, offers them support in Cinderline, and tells Cer Rantix that *Jimmy* at the Arena Shops can fix his disguise device permanently.

Jason fails to impress Don Fontaine.  The heroes retire to to their rooms and take a long rest, adding 2 to the Siphoning Track.

## Red and Black

Immediately before dawn, the players awake to crimson red Aurora Borealis traveling to Godfall Mountain.  Cer Rantix identifies it as unrecognizable healing magic, that is not necromantic in origin.

The heroes, traveling to the Arena Shops, discover that the obsidian black Cindersand burns their feet and the feet/hooves of their mounts.  While many individuals seem unaffected by it.  Perceiving that it seems to discriminate against the less fortunate.

## What lurks in the Arena

The heroes meet Jimmy--a bloodline slave to Don Fontaine, in what is an automotive mechanic shop with several crude Essentia powered automobiles.  They get Cer Rantix's device repaired.  Interested in what the heroes might have seen in the previous train heist, Jimmy shows Cer Rantix an Essentia Engine placed in a crude 100ft+ locomotive and asks hims about "Wyatt's" magic train.  Cer Rantix identifies the Essentia Engine as being an Essentia Soul Shard like the one in Ikeri (the Quarry Colossus).  Additionally, Jimmy tells them that in the Aristocrat district, is a cave where immunity to the Cindersand can be acquired.  This is only for the well connected.

The heroes attempt to travel into the guarded Aristocrat district only to be stopped by Ebert Warhol.  They then meet with Don Fontaine in his warehouse/office to find out how to gain entrance, and Don Fontaine suggests they leave it alone.  Pushing, Don Fontaine strikes a deal with the Heroes: Succeed in the Arena--a demolition derby--and he will arrange access to the cave.

Jason successfully convinces Don Fontaine to investigate the Warhol family, especially Ebert Warhol.
