# Session 4 - The forgotten episode

> *I forgot the writeup for this session.  This is a recap.*

Our players Nyx, Clint, and Cer Rantix, get on a train headed to Cinderline.  After most of the journey, the train is stopped in the desert by a group of men.  Suspecting their story about a broken down train ahead, the heroes investigate.

Clint uses his familiar to scout ahead and finds a see-saw train, managed by bandits blocking the path.

Travelling ahead with one of the suspect men, they do not find the see-saw train, but discover it has been moved further forward.  Confronting the bandits they take them out and then head back.

Nearly back to the train, a magic train barrels by with part of the party's train connected.

Through a series of crits <critical /> and great rolls, the players are (surprisingly!) able to board the train and confront the train bandits.  A battle ensues and the heroes discover that the cargo wagon holding their mounts are included in the stolen section of the train.

The heroes unlatch the stolen portion of the train, and the train bandits manage to escape.  The heroes survive the ensuing train crash by dodging, flying, etc. away from the tumbling cargo wagons.

<hr />

The players head back to their train and continue on to Cinderline with the reassurance that the train cargo--and their mounts--will be delivered to them.
